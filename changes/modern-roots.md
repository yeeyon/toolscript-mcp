- On a 2026-07-28 connection (`TOOLSCRIPT_PROTOCOL=auto`), the built-in Workspace takes the roots of the client: the first tool call is answered with a request for the roots (`input_required`), which a client that declares `roots` answers at once, and the root narrows as on the older protocol, never wider than the configured root. A client that declares no roots, or never answers, has no Workspace on that connection; `TOOLSCRIPT_PROTOCOL=legacy` gives it the configured root.
