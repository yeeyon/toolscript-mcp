- Workspace `list` and `search` keep one walk at a time in a session (issue #180). Two walks were measured against one in CI (Linux and Windows with 4 CPUs, trees of 2500, 5500 and 16000 files, and a container with 2 CPUs): a batch of 8 `search` calls took 3 % to 60 % less time with two, but on a tree where one search takes more than half of the 10 s budget, two walks slow each other to the budget and cut calls that one slot did not cut. The bench (`bench/walks-slots.sh`, `.github/workflows/bench.yml`, `bench/RESULTS-walks.md`) is new.
