- Workspace `list` and `search` now run two walks at one time in a session, not one (issue #180), and one on a machine with a single CPU. In a CI bench with 4 CPUs (Linux and Windows, trees of 2500 and 5500 files) and in a Docker container with 2 CPUs, a batch of 8 `search` calls in a `Promise.all` took 3 % to 60 % less time (median) and no call was cut. A search alone is not slower: its differences are inside the spread between passes (`bench/RESULTS-walks.md`).
