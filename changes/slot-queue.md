- Workspace `list` and `search` calls that wait for the one walk slot no longer end with a request timeout when many run at once (for example a `Promise.all` of 8 searches over 16,000 files). A call that still waits after the call limit (`sandbox.callTimeoutSeconds`) less 12 s (48 s by default) answers at once with `notStarted: true` and `truncated: true`, and the footer of the run says how many calls did not start and what to do. A call that got its slot still runs its whole 10 s. `WORKSPACE_VERSION` is 1.1.0.
